Welcome to Pansea

Located at 5 Vasileiou Chatzi in Thessaloniki, Pansea Exclusive Suites offers a central location with beachfront access. Each apartment features air-conditioning, a fully equipped kitchenette, and a private bathroom. Guests enjoy stunning sea views, a sun terrace, and free WiFi throughout the property.

With a remarkable rating of 9.4/10 from guests, our property combines comfort, style, and an unbeatable location near the Archaeological Museum, White Tower, and the vibrant city center.
